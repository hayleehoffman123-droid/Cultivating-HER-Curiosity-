The story behind the seed

Meet the Founder.

Haylee Hoffman

Growing curiosity into opportunity.

Cultivating HER Curiosity began with a belief that every young person deserves the chance to discover agriculture and imagine themselves as part of its future.

Haylee’s own experiences in agricultural education showed her how powerful it can be when someone is given an opportunity to learn by doing, encouraged to keep asking questions, and able to see people like themselves represented in the industry.

She created Cultivating HER Curiosity to bring those opportunities to more young people through the three HER pillars: Hands-On Learning, Education, and Representation.

“I don’t just want to give young people a seat at the table. I want to help make the table longer.”

Through community initiatives, youth activities, agricultural education, and partnerships, Haylee hopes to plant a seed of curiosity that can grow into confidence, passion, and a future in agriculture.

Beyond the field

Her goal: Help young people discover that agriculture is bigger than any one career, background, or path.

Her favorite part: Watching someone try something new and realize, “I can do this.”

Her reminder: Plant a Seed. Grow a Future.

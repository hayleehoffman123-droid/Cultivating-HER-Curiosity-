About the Initiative

Why Cultivating HER Curiosity?

Agriculture is stronger when more young people can picture themselves in it.

Cultivating HER Curiosity was created to turn agricultural education into experiences that feel welcoming, exciting, and possible.

The initiative focuses on giving young people opportunities to participate, learn, ask questions, discover careers, and see that there is no single picture of what an agriculturalist has to look like.

Our message

You belong in the future of agriculture.
